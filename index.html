How It Works

Simple. Secure. Transparent.

From listing to harvest — everything in one platform, with your funds protected every step of the way.

1
🌾
Farmers List Projects
Farmers create detailed project profiles with farm plans, expected yields, photo evidence, and projected ROI for investors to evaluate.
2
💳
Investors Fund Securely
Investors browse, choose, and fund projects via Paystack or Flutterwave. Funds are held in escrow until verified farm milestones are met.
3
📊
Track & Earn Returns
Both parties get real-time updates — progress photos, harvest reports, and automatic payouts when milestones are approved.
Platform Features

Built for Trust & Growth

Every feature is designed to protect farmers, give investors confidence, and keep money flowing back to communities.

🔒
Escrow Protection
Investor funds are held securely in escrow and only released to farmers when our admin team verifies each milestone — protecting everyone.
📸
Live Farm Updates
Farmers upload real-time progress photos and harvest reports. Investors see exactly what their money is growing, always.
💰
Multiple Investment Models
Choose donation-based giving, reward-based returns (receive produce), or profit-sharing equity — all in one platform.
📊
Investor Analytics
Track your full portfolio, expected returns, projected harvest dates, and ROI history from a clean, intuitive dashboard.
⭐
Farmer Ratings
Investors rate farmers after each project. A transparent rating system builds trust and surfaces the best farmers to the top.
🌍
Cross-Border Investing
Nigerian diaspora and international investors can fund projects from anywhere in the world via Stripe or Flutterwave.
